	<h3>Missions </h3>
	<p class="Missions">Time and Fate | Suffer With Me</p>
	<hr>

	<h2>Time and Fate</h2>
	<p>This mission starts with Alex Mason, Frank Woods, and Jason Hudson spying on Menendez as an attempt to capture him.
		The player take on control of Raul Menendez, the main enemy in the game, while he's in his room with his sleeping
		sister. The soldiers that Mason, Woods, and Hudson are allied with barge in and capture him. The camera cuts and
		Menendez is in hand cuffs in front of the leader of the soliders, Manuel Noriega. He uncuffs Menendez, helping him
		out. He jumps on Noriega and punches him unconcious. A distorting, red layer and the screams of his sister can be
		heard as Menendez takes his Spas-12 shotgun and machete and begins to carve through Noriega's forces. He makes it
		back to his the hallway to his apartment room and finds Mason, Woods, and Hudson on the other side. Then, the camera
		cuts and shows Masons perspective through the mission. When they get to the hallway, Woods, overcome with anger from
		being tortured, throws a grenade at Menendez, but the trajectory is changed by Mason who tries to stop him. The
		grenade ends up inside Menendez's room, killing his sister.</p>

	<h2>Suffer With Me</h2>
	<p>Mason and Woods are given the objective to capture Manuel Noriega by Hudson. However, just upon capturing him,
		Hudson changes objective. Noriega is to be deemed PC, or Precious Cargo, and taken through the dangerous streets of
		Nicaragua for a trade off. Near the end of the mission, Mason seperates from Woods. It is unclear where Mason goes
		at the time, but Woods takes an elevator up to the roof of a building to kill a target. Noriega acts very smug the
		whole time. Hudson informs Woods as he is aiming that the target is Raul Menendez. Woods aims and shoots "Menendez"
		non-lethally. Suspensful music plays as they walk toward the body. Noriega has a smug look on his face and Woods
		feels something is wrong. He walk faster and starts running toward the body and pulls the fabric from his face and
		reveals the true identity of the person he shot: Mason. Woods is knocked down by Menendez who shoots his knees with
		12 gauge and knocks him out.He, Mason, and Hudson now appear in a torture room with Menendez and David Mason, Alex's
		son. He says that one of the must die, and Hudson eventually volunteers. End Misson.</p>
